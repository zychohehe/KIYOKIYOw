# beatlyrics: credit

The lyrics sources in this module (lrc.red, BiniLyrics, BetterLyrics, PaxSenix, LyricsPlus, Unison,
SimpMusic, KuGou, LRCLIB, Musixmatch, Genius, Megalobiz) and their parsers were taken from
**Beatcord** (the project formerly named BitChord), by kushagrasinghx:
https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord

Beatcord is GPL-3.0, like Mochy. The package was renamed to `com.music.beatlyrics`, the YouTube
sources were left out (Kiyo has its own), and `Http.kt` is a small replacement for Beatcord's
app-wide HTTP client. Everything else is unchanged.

The lyrics themselves belong to their owners and are fetched live from third-party services.
Some of those services are community mirrors or unofficial endpoints, so check each service's
terms before publishing the app.
